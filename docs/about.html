About Ruby Jones

I'm an author and artist specialising in science fiction, fantasy, and horror smut. I like robots and magic and tentacles and butts, among a great many other things. I'm also agender, disabled, and depressed, and as a result my writing tends to reflect a sympathy for outcasts and marginalised people. I like to take someone who has been othered and subject them to love and acceptance.

Before I got sick with myalgic encephalomylitis/chronic fatigue syndrome (ME/CFS) I studied philosophy, with research interests in philosophical scepticism and the philosophy of mind. I was fascinated by computational theory of mind, and my reflections on the nature of experience and mindedness informs my writing. I hope this adds a little extra interest to my android stories.

When illness made it impossible to go on in academia, I worked for a while as an editor and proofreader, before moving into marketing, and finally technical writing. Then, in spring 2020, I caught COVID and my health became significantly worse. I had to stop work entirely in 2023.

Writing has always been my escape, and as I was clinging on to a working life I became incredibly prolific; I wrote more smut, and at the end of 2020 I finally bit the bullet and published my first erotic story, Oviposition. I began cleaning up longer works for publication, drew the cover for Ruinous Attraction, and I set up a Medium blog where I started sharing short stories and poetry.

My progress as an indie writer has been full of stops and starts with the fluctuations of my health, but I keep pressing forward. If you'd like to support me, please consider signing up to my Patreon, where I try to produce semi-regular content, including a series of posts on marketing for independent authors. You can also buy cover art and other artworks from me in my Redbubble store.
