About Ruby Jones

I'm an author and artist specialising in science fiction, fantasy, and horror smut. I likes robots and magic and tentacles butts, among a great many other things. I'm also agender, disabled and depressed, and as a result my writing tends to reflect a sympathy for outcasts and marginalised people. I have a particular hankering for angst and unothering the other - taking someone who has been excluded and subjecting them to love and acceptance.

In another life, I was an analytic philosopher, with research interests in epistemology and metaphysics, particularly philosophical scepticism and the philosophy of mind. I've studied computational theory of mind and this informs my writing when I imagine what the lived experience of an android would be like - and what it would be like for them to have sex...

In 2012, while I was studying for my PhD, I became sick with myalgic encephalomyelitis/chronic fatigue syndrome (ME/CFS), and although I would not be diagnosed for seven years, my health increasingly declined. Despite finishing my PhD with no corrections, I was unable to continue on in academia. I worked as an editor and proofreader, and spent a few years in marketing before becoming a tech writer in 2018. In spring 2020, I caught COVID and became significantly worse, stopping work entirely in 2023.

Writing has always been my escape, and as I was clinging on to a working life I became incredibly prolific; I wrote more smut - smut that was surprisingly popular - and at the end of 2020 I finally bit the bullet and published my first erotic story, Oviposition. I began cleaning up longer works for publication, drawing a cover for Ruinous Attraction, and I set up a Medium blog where I started sharing short stories and poetry.

I've since set up a Patreon, where I am trying to produce semi-regular content, as my health allows, including a series of posts on marketing for independent authors. I make all my own covers and have a guide on how you can make yours. You can also buy cover art and other artworks from me in my Redbubble store.
