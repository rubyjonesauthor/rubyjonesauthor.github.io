About Ruby Jones

I'm an author and artist specialising in science fiction, fantasy, and horror smut. I like robots and magic and tentacles and butts, among a great many other things. I'm also agender, disabled, and depressed, and my writing tends to reflect a sympathy for outcasts and marginalised people.

In 2012, when I was studying for my PhD, I got sick with myalgic encephalomylitis/chronic fatigue syndrome (ME/CFS). I wouldn't be diagnosed until 2019, but my health steadily deteriorated, and I wasn't able to go on in academia after I graduated. Nevertheless, you will still find hints of my research interests in epistemology and the philosophy of mind in most of my works - especially those involving androids.

I worked for a while as an editor and proofreader, before moving into marketing, and finally technical writing. In spring 2020, I caught COVID and my health got much worse. I had to stop work entirely in 2023.

Writing has always been my escape, and as I was clinging on to a working life I became incredibly prolific. At the end of 2020 published my first erotic story, Oviposition. I began cleaning up longer works for publication, drew the cover for Ruinous Attraction, and set up a Medium blog, where I started sharing short stories and poetry.

My progress as an indie writer has been full of stops and starts with the fluctuations of my health, but I keep pressing forward. If you'd like to support me, please consider signing up to my Patreon, where I try to produce semi-regular content, including advice for other independent authors.

Anti-LLM policy

Despite my love of fictional artifical intelligence and theorising about machine minds, I have a strong aversion to what is currently being marketed as 'AI'. Which is to say: large language models (LLMs) trained on stolen writing and similar neural nets trained on stolen art, to the overwhelming detriment of our environment, climate, and knowledge ecosystem. I will never knowingly use such models to generate my stories, writing, or other content. I assume you want to read my books to connect with someone else's thoughts and feelings; stories happen where minds meet.
